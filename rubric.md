# Scoring Rubric v1

Score each posting 0–10. The score reflects fit for a junior/middle Python backend
developer (~2 yrs production) based in Tbilisi, Georgia, seeking remote work.

## Scoring axes

| Axis | Weight | 10 | 5 | 0 |
|------|--------|----|---|---|
| Stack match | 30% | Python-primary backend (FastAPI, Django, Celery, async) | Python present but secondary or full-stack | No Python / Java-primary / Go-primary |
| Seniority fit | 25% | Junior or mid-level explicitly | Unspecified / "2-4 yrs" range | Senior/Staff/Principal only |
| Industry | 20% | Fintech, crypto, blockchain infra | Other tech (SaaS, marketplace, dev-tools) | Non-tech (healthcare, education, govt) |
| Location eligibility | 15% | Fully remote / EMEA / "anywhere" | Remote but timezone-restricted (US hours OK if flexible) | US-only / EU-only with no Georgia option |
| Salary signal | 10% | Listed and competitive ($40k+) | Not listed | Listed and below $30k |

## Hard disqualifiers (score = 0 regardless)

- Requires 5+ years experience or says "Senior"/"Staff"/"Principal" with no mid option
- Python is incidental (Go/Java/C# is the primary language)
- US-only or EU-only with work authorization required
- Management/lead role with no IC track
- Not a software engineering role (data science only, DevOps only, QA only)

## Scoring formula

Sum weighted axis scores, round to nearest integer.

## Worked examples

**Posting:** "Python Backend Engineer" at a crypto exchange, 2-4 yrs, fully remote, $60-80k
- Stack: 10 (Python primary, backend)
- Seniority: 10 (2-4 yrs = mid)
- Industry: 10 (crypto)
- Location: 10 (fully remote)
- Salary: 10 (listed, competitive)
- **Final: 10**

**Posting:** "Senior Full-Stack Engineer (React + Django)" at a healthcare startup, US-only
- Hard disqualifier: "Senior" + "US-only"
- **Final: 0**
